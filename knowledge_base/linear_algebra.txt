Q2. What is a span in linear algebra? What is a matrix linear algebra? What is subspace linear algebra?    
The set of all feasible linear combinations of a set of vectors is referred to as the span of that set in linear algebra. The vector space holding the initial vectors is always the subspace of the span. A collection of vectors is said to cover the complete vector space if its span encompasses every vector in the vector space. A collection of vectors is said to be linearly dependent if its span is a proper subspace—that is if it contains just a portion of the vector space.  In linear algebra, matrices are used to express linear mappings and enable explicit calculations. The study of matrices is a substantial portion of linear algebra, and most characteristics and actions of abstract linear algebra may be described in terms of matrices. 
In the field of mathematics, particularly in linear algebra, a vector space that is a subset of a broader vector space is referred to as a linear subspace or vector subspace.

Any element of vector space V that can be uniquely expressed as a finite linear mix of elements of set B is referred to as a basis in maths. The dimension of the subspace that the eigenvectors linked to λ span are known as the geometric multiplicity. A m × n matrix A's null space, sometimes referred to as Null A, is the collection of every possible answer to the homogeneous equation Ax = 0.

Q4. What is a trivial solution in linear algebra? What is a vector space in linear algebra?
If every coordinate in a vector is zero, or if the vector is the zero vector, it is referred to as trivial. A vector space, often referred to as a linear space, is an assembly of vectors multiplied ("scaled") by scalars or integers, then combined together. Typically, scalars are regarded as real numbers.
